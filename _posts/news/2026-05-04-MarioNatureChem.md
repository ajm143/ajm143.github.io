Glass modification principles have been used to transform silicate glasses into the everyday materials we use today. We adopt these same principles, specifically modification using alkali ions, to see if they affect the emerging class of metal-organic framework (MOF) glasses in the same way. In our joint computational/experimental investigation with NMR spectroscopy, we applied our previous technique of develpoing amorphous models with AIRSS, where we found likely environments within the framework material for our alkali-ion modifiers. By sitting in metal-site vacancies, the modifiers reduce the connectivity and increase the distortion of the framework, resulting in a lowered glass transition temperature that promises to improve MOF glasses' overall processibility.
